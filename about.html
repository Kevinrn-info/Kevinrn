Facts

Throughout my tenure as a Data Analytics Project Lead at Servicios PSI Internacional S.A., I drove data governance and operational maintenance to maximize business value and enable strategic, data-driven decisions across multiple U.S. state highway systems. To achieve low-latency insights, I spearheaded the implementation of Pathway, a high-performance, real-time stream processing and ETL framework. By leveraging its Rust-powered engine and unified batch-streaming architecture, I successfully built reactive data pipelines capable of processing continuous traffic logs and state-level infrastructure telemetry, eliminating data staleness and transforming raw operational flows into actionable decisions for state stakeholders.

As a project lead, I steered teams of up to 10 professionals to evaluate highway infrastructure across 14+ states. We deployed 5 different analysis and insight frameworks to process data for over 650,000 miles of roadway. Ultimately, we turned massive infrastructure telemetry into millions of high-accuracy data points that now fuel critical, daily decisions for Department of Transportation (DOT) agencies.
